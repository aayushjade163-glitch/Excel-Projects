# 🛒 Project 1: BlinkIT Grocery Sales & Outlet Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes grocery sales data to understand product performance, outlet characteristics, sales distribution, and customer ratings. An Excel dashboard was developed to present key performance indicators and support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total sales and average sales performance.
- Evaluate sales across different item types and fat-content categories.
- Compare outlet performance by location tier, size, and type.
- Explore item visibility and its relationship with product performance.
- Monitor customer ratings and key business metrics.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $1.20M |
| Average Sales per Item | $140.99 |
| Number of Items | 8,523 |
| Average Rating | 3.97 |

*Note: Verify the source dataset's currency and formatting before interpreting the sales value.*

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- **Item Analysis:** Evaluated sales across product categories and fat-content groups.
- **Outlet Analysis:** Compared sales by outlet type, size, establishment year, and location tier.
- **Sales Analysis:** Examined total and average sales across product and outlet segments.
- **Visibility Analysis:** Explored item visibility as a product-performance dimension.
- **Customer Rating Analysis:** Reviewed average ratings across the dataset.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for important business metrics.
- Pivot-based charts for product and outlet comparisons.
- Interactive slicers and filters.
- Consolidated sales performance dashboard.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard helps summarize grocery sales performance, compare outlet segments, and identify product categories that deserve further investigation. It demonstrates how Excel can support retail reporting and business intelligence.

## 📁 Project File

# 🍔 Project 2: Swiggy Food Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Swiggy food-order data to explore sales performance, food categories, restaurant activity, geographical patterns, and customer ratings. An Excel dashboard was created to convert raw data into an organized business reporting solution.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total sales and average order value.
- Explore restaurant and dish-level performance.
- Compare food categories and vegetarian versus non-vegetarian items.
- Examine sales across cities, states, and localities.
- Analyze customer ratings and rating counts.
- Explore time-based patterns using order dates.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales Value | ₹5.30 Crore |
| Average Rating | 4.34 |
| Total Rating Count | 5.59 Million |
| Number of Dataset Records | 197,430 |
| Average Order Value | Approximately ₹268.49 |

*Note: These values reflect the workbook's analysis. The number of dataset records should not be treated as unique orders unless the data confirms that each record represents a separate transaction.*

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- **Sales Analysis:** Examined overall sales value and average order value.
- **Restaurant Analysis:** Explored restaurant-level performance and food offerings.
- **Food Category Analysis:** Compared food categories and their contribution to sales.
- **Veg vs. Non-Veg Analysis:** Compared vegetarian and non-vegetarian food segments.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Examined performance across states, cities, and localities.
- **Time-Based Analysis:** Explored order patterns by day, week, and quarter.
- **Customer Rating Analysis:** Reviewed average ratings and rating counts.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for overall performance.
- Charts comparing food categories and locations.
- Restaurant and dish-level analysis.
- Veg versus non-veg comparisons.
- Time-based analysis of order activity.
- Consolidated food sales and customer insights dashboard.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard helps summarize food sales, compare category and location performance, and explore customer feedback and order trends. It demonstrates the use of Excel for food-delivery analytics and business reporting.
